# VELOX — AS-IS vs. TO-BE Comparison

## 1. Purpose

This comparison evaluates the expected impact of the proposed TO-BE process against the current AS-IS process.

The TO-BE figures are modeled targets for the fictional VELOX case study. They are not measurements from a real company.

## 2. KPI Comparison

| KPI                         |      AS-IS | TO-BE Target |          Expected Improvement |
| --------------------------- | ---------: | -----------: | ----------------------------: |
| Total Order Processing Time | 12.0 hours |    6.0 hours |                 50% reduction |
| Payment Verification Time   |  2.5 hours |   0.25 hours |                 90% reduction |
| Inventory Check Time        |  1.5 hours |   0.25 hours |                 83% reduction |
| Warehouse Picking Time      |  3.0 hours |    1.5 hours |                 50% reduction |
| Packing Time                |   1.0 hour |    0.5 hours |                 50% reduction |
| Inventory Error Rate        |         8% |           3% |               62.5% reduction |
| First-Time-Right Rate       |        91% |          97% |   6 percentage-point increase |
| Manual Processing Rate      |        70% |          30% | 40 percentage-point reduction |
| Order Delay Rate            |        18% |           7% |               61.1% reduction |
| Cost per Order              |      €8.50 |        €6.00 |               29.4% reduction |

## 3. Key Improvements

### Processing Time

The modeled target reduces total order processing time from 12 hours to 6 hours.

This represents an expected **50% reduction** in the time required to process an order.

### Manual Work

The modeled manual processing rate decreases from 70% to 30%.

This means employees can spend more time on exception handling and higher-value activities instead of repetitive verification.

### Inventory Accuracy

The inventory error rate is targeted to decrease from 8% to 3%.

The reduction is expected to decrease rework and prevent avoidable fulfillment delays.

### Order Delays

The order delay rate is targeted to decrease from 18% to 7%.

This should improve fulfillment reliability and the customer experience.

### Cost per Order

The modeled cost per order decreases from €8.50 to €6.00.

This represents a potential saving of €2.50 per processed order.

## 4. Expected Business Impact

If VELOX processes 2,000 orders per month, the modeled cost reduction would be:

**2,000 × €2.50 = €5,000 monthly savings**

Annualized:

**€5,000 × 12 = €60,000 potential annual savings**

These figures are illustrative estimates based on the fictional VELOX assumptions and should not be interpreted as actual company savings.

## 5. Management Summary

The proposed TO-BE process is designed to address the major inefficiencies identified in the AS-IS process.

The largest expected improvements are:

* 50% faster order processing.
* 90% faster payment verification.
* 83% faster inventory checks.
* 62.5% lower inventory error rate.
* 61.1% lower order delay rate.
* 29.4% lower modeled cost per order.
* €60,000 potential annual savings at 2,000 orders per month.

The analysis suggests that automation, real-time data synchronization, and improved workflow management could significantly improve VELOX's fulfillment process.

## 6. Important Assumption

VELOX is a fictional business created for this portfolio project.

All AS-IS measurements and TO-BE targets are modeled assumptions designed to demonstrate business-process analysis techniques.

The next stage of the project will use synthetic process data to test these assumptions and demonstrate how the KPIs could be monitored in practice.
